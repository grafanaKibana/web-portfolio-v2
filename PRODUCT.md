# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring companies and recruiters evaluating Nikita Reshetnik for AI and software engineering work are the primary audience. Engineering peers, prospective collaborators, and others interested in his work may also use the site.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio is an interactive, extended résumé. It lets visitors scan Nikita's experience, then explore details that would not fit in a conventional résumé. A visitor should be able to understand relevant work, inspect supporting projects or writing, and contact Nikita directly.

## Positioning

The site connects a résumé-style career overview with deeper, navigable evidence: experience details, project case studies, technical writing, and questions about the published portfolio content. Those layers let a visitor investigate a specific skill or role fit without making the short résumé carry every detail.

## Operating Context

Visitors may review the site quickly on desktop or mobile, read a case study or article in depth, download the linked résumé, follow public profile links, or open an email to make contact. The site-wide conversation offers another way to ask about Nikita's published work; contact and navigation remain available without it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home presents a career overview, experience, education, skills, selected projects, code activity, writing, and contact links. Project and article routes provide deeper reading.
- Experience details can expand in place. The conversation answers portfolio-scoped questions with links to validated site sources; it does not browse job URLs or store a server-side transcript.
- Structured records and local MDX own published biographical and project facts. Optional public activity and plugin metadata must not replace curated content when unavailable.
- Contact opens the visitor's email app; the site does not claim a message was delivered.
- Audience priority beyond hiring reviewers, and any quantitative success measure, remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name “Nikita Reshetnik” and the existing N/R mark. Published copy speaks in Nikita's first person: factual, conversational, and specific about work rather than promotional. Preserve source-backed career claims and avoid invented credentials, outcomes, or scale.

## Evidence on Hand

- [Structured portfolio records](content/portfolio.yaml) include experience, education, skills, recommendations, links, and selected content.
- [Project case studies](content/projects/) and [technical articles](content/articles/) provide extended examples of work.
- [Public brand mark](public/brand/mark.svg), local project images, company marks, and certification assets support the published record.
- The linked résumé and public profiles provide additional paths for verification; their external contents can change independently of this repository.

## Product Principles

1. Make the main career story understandable at a glance, with deeper details available by choice.
2. Ground every professional claim in authored records or linked evidence.
3. Keep reading, navigation, and direct contact useful when optional data or interactive enhancements fail.
4. Let visitors investigate the parts of Nikita's work relevant to their own question.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard use, visible focus, semantic reading order, and reduced-motion preferences. Keep essential content and navigation available without JavaScript, and do not make hover the only way to reach information.
